### R1 — HIGH, blocking — A3 is real, on a live panel path, and identical in extent to A1

`src/reachy_ai/motion/rig_routes.py:814-816` still says *"WAVE, LIFT_TO_PRESENT
and POINT are absent on purpose: none of them lands the arm on REST or
departs from it"*, and `tests/unit/test_rig_routes.py:425` pins it. Sonnet was
told to leave A3 alone; it did. But the design in §3 cannot be built on a
guard with this hole, so it is the first repair.

**It is a live path, not a notebook curiosity.** `web/motion_worker.py:231-248`
flies an *approach* whenever the arm is not at the ability's expected start:
`wave` or `point_*` requested while the arm rests on the board resolves
`R.path("rest", "present") == ["LIFT_TO_PRESENT"]` and flies it. The
executor's superset check (`panel_executor.py:512-522`) for `end_posture ==
"present"` unions `RAISE_TO_SIDE`, `LIFT_TO_PRESENT` and `WAVE`; only
`RAISE_TO_SIDE` is in `FOOTPRINT_LEGS`. So a wave from REST is guarded
against the *pocket* corridor's tail and not against the lift it actually
flies.

**Quantified (Probe, `probe_board_sweep.py`).** 4 cm grid over the whole
table top, standing box, 13 samples per leg (the guard's own sampling), in
both the capsule model (A2 rule) and the MJCF shell model:

| object | leg | positions negative on the leg **and** clear on the checked REST→REST_SHUT→HOVER tail |
|---|---|---|
| 6 cm cube | PRESENT→REST_SHUT descent (A1) | capsule 5, shells 2 |
| 6 cm cube | REST→PRESENT lift (A3) | capsule 5, shells 2 |
| 6×6×12 cm block | descent (A1) | capsule 8, shells 4 |
| 6×6×12 cm block | lift (A3) | capsule 8, shells 4 |

The A3 set is the A1 set (the lift is the descent reversed; REST and
REST_SHUT differ only in the gripper). Example, 12 cm block at
x=0.23 y=−0.32 (near-right corner strip of the board, right of column 3, row
1): lift −2.8 cm, checked tail +1.0 cm — **in both models**, i.e. it is the
forearm/upper arm, whose capsules are exact, not the over-wide hand.

Fix: `FOOTPRINT_LEGS["LIFT_TO_PRESENT"] = (REST, PRESENT)`; correct the
comment and the test; add a board-height regression case (below). This makes
the guard *more* conservative, is justified by both models, and needs no
measurement. WAVE's exclusion, by contrast, is supportable with a number: its
three legs vs a 12 cm block anywhere on the board read worst **+1.6 cm**
(tube hand, fully open, `probe_wave_legs.txt`) — thin, at the same corner
strip, but positive; record the number instead of "cannot catch an object".

### 3.2 The evidence-board disagreement, resolved

`probe_evidence_board.py` puts ADR-0002's board (`soda_can`@r1c1,
`foam_block`@r2c3, placed by `ObjectPlacer.place`) into the MuJoCo scene and
walks every `FOOTPRINT_LEGS` leg at the guard's 13 samples (and 101), reading
three geometries at each sample:

| quantity | foam_block@r2c3, worst over any guarded route | closest part |
|---|---|---|
| capsule, always-open hand (pre-A2) | **−2.6 cm** | hand tube |
| capsule, A2 commanded aperture | **−1.7 cm** (OPEN legs) / +0.5 (SHUT legs) | hand tube |
| MJCF **collision** geoms (what "board undisturbed" is measured against) | **+4.5 … +4.8 cm** | `r_forearm_col` |
| MJCF **visual shells** (`r_thumb_body`, `r_finger_body`, `r_wrist_ball`) + arm capsules | **+4.5 … +4.8 cm** | `r_forearm_col`; thumb shell ≥ +6.0, finger ≥ +9.1 |
| 13 vs 101 samples | identical to 0.1 cm on every tabletop leg | — |

So the three numbers the 2026-09-11 review could not reconcile are one
model error:

- **The upper-arm and forearm capsules are the physics.** `kinematics.py:125-130`
  copies `r_upper_arm_col` / `r_forearm_col` (capsule 0.035×0.28, 0.030×0.25)
  exactly; at the rail, capsule forearm +2.68 cm vs MuJoCo +2.7 cm at the
  same sample (Ran).
- **The hand capsule is not.** It is one isotropic tube, 14.5 cm long, radius
  5.2–8.3 cm by aperture, around two shells that are 5×5.6×7.6 cm (thumb) and
  2.4×2×7.6 cm (finger). On the evidence board the tube reaches 1.7–2.6 cm
  *into* `foam_block` while the thumb shell is 6.0 cm from it. Every
  documented model-vs-physics disagreement examined is the tube: the
  evidence board; SWING_1's "6 mm graze" (tube hand +0.64 cm shut, −2.45 open,
  while the MJCF's tightest part there is the *forearm* at +2.7 cm and the
  thumb shell is ≥ +3.7 cm); #73's "tucked sweep that flies at −5 cm modelled".
- **The physics hand is smaller than the real hand.** The MJCF's collision
  hand is two fingertip boxes (`r_thumb_col` 2.8×1.6×4.4 cm, `r_finger_col`
  2.8×1.6×2.8 cm); the palm, wrist ball and shell bodies have
  `contype=0`. **The simulator cannot report palm or wrist contact.** "Board
  undisturbed" over eighteen legs is evidence about the fingertips, forearm
  and upper arm — not about the hand's body. Neither the tube nor the
  collision boxes is the truth; the shells are the project's own best
  description of the hand and sit between them.
- **The eighteen flights are consistent** with the collision-geom and shell
  readings (+4.5…+4.8 cm) and inconsistent only with the tube.
- **#74's "+12.2 cm closest approach"** was measured on the 2026-09-10
  flights with `soda_can` and `foam_block` "on the board" at unrecorded
  cells, by a script that is not in the repository, with an unknown hand
  aperture. With `foam_block` on r2c3 the same tube would have read
  ≤ −0.4 cm at REST, so those objects were not on r2c3. It cannot be
  reconciled from the record and is superseded by the r1c1/r2c3 re-flight;
  the number that flight supports is +4.5…+4.8 cm (physics geometry at the
  commanded poses).

Across a 4 cm grid of 6 cm cubes over the whole board (224 positions × 13
samples, descent leg) the tube is **never** less conservative than the
arm-only shells (0/224) and over-reads by up to **9.3 cm**; it disagrees in

These are four different measurements and the record has been mixing them:

1. **Predicted geometric clearance** (commanded pose, model). Exact for the
   upper arm and forearm; 0–9 cm too pessimistic for the hand (§3.2).
2. **Tracking error** (commanded vs realised joints, through the move). The
   only through-the-move data are the rail rows: tube hand planned +0.64 cm
   at SWING_1, realised −0.33…−1.58 cm → **1.0–2.2 cm of degradation**

   mid-move pose is off the joint-space line). Yesterday's panel episodes add

3. **Simulator contact** — `data.contact` between collision geoms only. Not
   available for the hand's body at all; `mj_geomDistance` on box–box pairs
   is unreliable (R9).
4. **"Undisturbed"** — the object moved less than `OBJECT_DRIFT_TOL = 0.02`

   moves nothing is invisible; a fingertip pass-through of the palm region is

### 3.6 Concurrent-reset contract (proposal)

Failing case (reproduced, `TestConcurrentResetCoalescing`): two
`submit_reset` calls in one sim tick → one physical reset, one ack, the
earlier asker's `request_id` never acknowledged → for the Docker bridge
(`mujoco_remote_backend.py:304-322`) RESETTING → 5 s timeout → ABORTED while
holding every joint command.

Proposed contract — **one physical reset per tick, every coalesced asker
acknowledged**:

- `SimState._pending_resets: list` (bounded, e.g. 8; overflow refuses the
  newest with an error ack, mirroring `submit_place`).
- `apply_pending()` applies **one** reset using the **newest** submission's
  `seed`/`jitter_m` (newest-wins is the slot's existing semantics; log a
  warning when coalesced seeds differ) and returns a list of
  `{request_id, _conn_id}` for all of them.
- `_sim_thread` builds one `ResetAck` per entry — each with its own
  `request_id`, the same `sim_step` and `scene_revision` — and routes each
  through `_deliver_reset_ack`. No protocol change: `ResetAck` already carries
  one `request_id`, and the bridge matches on it.
- Meaning of the ack: "a reset you asked for has completed, at this step".
  True for every asker. The only lie would be about parameters, which the
  warning covers and the bridge never sends.

Tests: two submissions, one `apply_pending` → both request_ids acked at the
same `sim_step`, `objects.reset` called once (spy), queue for each asker
holds exactly its own ack; overflow refuses. This is settled and offline —
it can go to Sonnet as a separate task at any time.

### 3.7 Unsupported geometry and uncertain scene state (current gaps)

- **Orientation is never live.** `web/panel_sim_link.py:343-360` keeps
  `pos_xyz` and drops the `quat_wxyz` the server streams
  (`native_mujoco/objects.py:100-108`); `SceneModel.update_poses` updates
  centres only. A toppled 11.5 cm can is modelled as an upright cylinder at
  its live centre. Fix is plumbing: carry the quaternion into `SimSnapshot`
  / `ObjectView` and into `update_poses`. Until then, an object whose live
  orientation is unknown should be checked as the upright cylinder of its
  **longest** extent (that is a bound, not a margin).
- **Objects without a live centre are silently checked at their YAML pose**
  (`panel_executor.py:560-561`; in SivaPool that is the floor). The guard
  should refuse when any tracked manipulable is missing from the snapshot,
  and when the snapshot is older than the executor's own freshness window.
- **Kinds.** `object_sdf` handles box/plane in their own frame and treats
  everything else as an upright cylinder of `footprint_radius`. Meshes and
  compound bodies are not represented; a scene that declares one should be
  refused by the guard rather than approximated.

### 3.8 Sampled vs swept, for the record

On the tabletop legs 13 vs 101 samples agree to 0.1 cm; at the rail leg by
2 mm. That supports recording "sampled at 13 per leg for legs of ≤ 45° joint
travel" as accepted — for **geometry**. It says nothing about the realised
path bowing off the joint-space line, which is a tracking effect (§3.3) and
is what actually hit the can in #56's account.

## 4. Recommended first #56/#74 slice

**Is a shared clearance *policy* justified now?** A shared **geometry** is:
the hand model is the common defect of every capsule consumer and can be
validated against the model file with no assumption. A shared **margin** is
not: no through-the-move realised data exist for the tabletop legs, the rail
data are tube readings, and the one pointing datum shows 3.5 cm of
degradation. The earlier review's proposed policy object with two margins
(5 cm tabletop, −1.6 cm rig) is **not** adopted: −1.6 cm is ADR-0002's
revisit tripwire at one rail under one model, not a penetration allowance,
and 5 cm has no measurement behind it for these legs.
